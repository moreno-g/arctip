TOKEN — $TIP

Utility tied to the product, not to speculation

Every mechanic answers a real need for a creator or a fan — nothing here exists just to exist.

Fee rebate

Creators holding $TIP in their wallet pay 0% on every tip received, instead of the standard 2%.

Holder cashback

Every tip returns a small percentage in $TIP to the fan who sent it.

Support tier

Top supporters unlock a visible badge on the creator's page, verified in $TIP.

Curation vote

The Discover page for emerging creators is voted on by $TIP holders.
