CIRCLE DEVELOPER PLATFORM INTEGRATION

Built for the Circle & USDC Ecosystem

ArcTip leverages Circle's native infrastructure to provide the smoothest micro-payment experience in Web3.

⚡

USDC Native Gas on Arc

Tips execute via native msg.value transfers on Arc. Fans hold USDC, tip USDC, and pay gas in USDC — no ERC-20 approval or secondary token needed.

🌐

Cross-Chain via Circle CCTP

Fans on Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum, or Base can tip seamlessly. Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP) burns USDC on source and mints natively to the creator's Arc handle.

🔑

Circle Programmable Wallets

Non-crypto fans log in effortlessly via Email, Google, or Passkeys powered by Circle Wallets — removing seed phrase friction completely.

🧩

Embeddable Creator Widgets

Creators get instant HTML & Markdown "Tip with USDC" badges to paste into GitHub READMEs, personal blogs, Linktree, and Twitch stream overlays.
