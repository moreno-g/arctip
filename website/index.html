What $TIP is actually for

$TIP launches with Arc mainnet, so none of this is running today. Here's what it will do — and why each piece earns its place instead of existing to be traded.

Fee rebate

Hold $TIP and you'll pay nothing on the tips you receive, instead of the standard 2%.

Holder cashback

Every tip will send a small percentage in $TIP back to the fan who paid it.

Support tier

Your biggest supporters will get a badge on your page, verified in $TIP.

Curation vote

$TIP holders will vote on which emerging creators get featured.
