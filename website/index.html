TRANSPARENT PRICING · 100% USDC

Simple, predictable USDC payouts

No custom tokens or speculative reward schemes — ArcTip settles everything transparently in USDC.

99% lands directly

The creator's share never touches an ArcTip balance. The contract splits the payment and pays the creator's wallet in the same transaction.

1%, capped in the contract

MAX_FEE_BPS is a constant: the fee can never be raised above 5%, by us or by anyone else. Every tip also carries the highest fee its sender agreed to, so a raise mid-flight reverts instead of overcharging.

No token, and none planned

Nothing to buy, hold, or stake. Tips are USDC, the fee is USDC, payouts are USDC. There is no ArcTip token and the contract has no mechanism to mint one.

Where the 1% goes

It exists to cover the gas ArcTip sponsors on behalf of fans, not to make a margin — a sponsored tip costs about 0.0039 USDC, and 1% of a 1 USDC tip is 0.01. The treasury address is in the deployment record and readable on-chain.
