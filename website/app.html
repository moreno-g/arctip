ARCTIP · TESTNET APP

Claim your page

Connect the wallet you want tips paid to, then claim a handle. Handles are first-come, first-served and can't be reassigned.

Running on Arc Testnet (chain 5042002). You'll need testnet USDC in your wallet — get some free from faucet.circle.com.

1. Connect wallet

This is the wallet your tips get paid to. Connect one you already have, or make one on this device with a passkey.

A passkey wallet lives on this device, unlocked with your fingerprint or face. Keep the passkey and you keep the wallet — losing it means losing the handle and the tips paid to it, so a creator earning real money should move to a wallet they back up.

2. Claim your handle

This is the name in your tip link — arctip.app/@you. Choose carefully.
